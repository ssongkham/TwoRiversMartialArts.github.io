Two Rivers Martial Arts · Central Iowa

Traditional Tae Kwon Do, taught by volunteers, at six branches across central Iowa.

We are a nonprofit club, not a franchise. Come and watch a class, or take one — the first is free, there is nothing to sign, and nobody will call you afterwards.

What we teach, besides technique

The Five Tenets of Tae Kwon Do

Read them in full →

예의

Ye Ui

Courtesy

Be polite, be fair, and respect the people you train with.

염치

Yom Chi

Integrity

Know right from wrong — and be honest about which one you chose.

인내

In Nae

Perseverance

Set the goal, then keep coming back. Patience leads to merit.

극기

Guk Gi

Self-Control

Work within your limits, in the do-jang and outside it.

백절불굴

Baekjul Boolgool

Indomitable Spirit

Speak and stand for what is right, whatever the odds.

A club, not a business

Two Rivers is a nonprofit run by its own members. Every instructor is a volunteer — they hold rank, they teach the class you walk into, and none of them is paid to do it. The dues cover the hall and the insurance, and that is why they are twenty-five dollars.

There are no contracts, no belt-testing packages, and no upgrade path to a more expensive class. Our lineage runs through Master Eric Heintz to Grandmaster Woo Jin Jung, and we still teach it the way it was handed to us.
