Our Beginning

Two Rivers traces its roots to the Eric Heintz Black Belt Academy, and through Master Heintz, to Grandmaster Woo Jin Jung of Jung's Tae Kwon Do in Cedar Rapids.

Master Heintz ran a traditional Tae Kwon Do school in the typical manner. He was one of Master Jung's best students and taught Tae Kwon Do at the YMCA.

A club, not a business

Two Rivers is a nonprofit run by its own members. Every instructor is a volunteer — they hold rank, they teach the class you walk into, and none of them is paid to do it. The dues cover the hall and the insurance, and that is why they are twenty-five dollars.

There are no contracts, no belt-testing packages, and no upgrade path to a more expensive class. We still teach it the way it was handed to us.
