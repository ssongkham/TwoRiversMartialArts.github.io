Resources

What students need, in one place.

Reference material for anyone training at Two Rivers — the tenets, the Korean, the forms and the belts — plus the club's photo and video archive going back to 1994.

For students

Tenets of Tae Kwon Do

Courtesy, Integrity, Perseverance, Self-Control and Indomitable Spirit — the five in full, with the Korean and the passages they come from.

Terminology

The official terminology sheet handed out to students: the Korean used in class and what it means.

Forms

The pattern required at each grade, written out move by move with the step count.

Significance of belts

What each of the seven belts stands for, from white through yellow, orange, green, blue and brown to black.

Dress code

How the dobok is worn, and what is expected of students in the do-jang.

Test schedule

Promotion tests are usually the first Saturday of each even month, held at the Hub. Times and make-up dates are posted here.

Curriculum

What is taught at each grade, and what a student is expected to know before testing.

Dues and fees

Monthly dues by branch, family rates, uniform cost and the testing fee for each grade.

Black belt essays

Essays written by members on the way to black belt. Hosted separately, at essays.trma.us.

Archive

Photo archive

Albums from tournaments, seminars and demonstrations — Big Brothers Big Sisters, Grinnell, Pil Sung, the New Year lock-in and more.

Video archive

Footage from 1994 to 2015, including Master Eric Heintz and Grandmasters McBroom, Gonzalez and Deaton.

Gallery

Photographs sorted by programme — Tae Kwon Do, Kobudo, Martial Spirit, self defence.
